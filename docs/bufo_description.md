# Bufo — Canonical Character Description

## Character brief (for text-to-video prompting)

Bufo is a small cartoon toad rendered in a soft, painterly digital-illustration style — gently shaded brushwork rather than flat vector art, with no hard black outlines. His body is a single squat, rounded blob: a wide toad silhouette with no visible neck, the broad head merging directly into a plump torso that tapers to a small base. Proportions are compact and front-heavy, roughly as wide as he is tall, sitting low like a resting toad.

His skin is a muted, earthy mossy green (desaturated olive-green midtones around #507040–#608040), with darker olive shading on his back and sides and a paler sage-green chin, throat, and belly. The texture reads as smooth and slightly matte, with soft bumpy toad-like contours — not glossy.

Two large, round, glossy eyes sit high and wide apart on top of his head, nearly touching: yellow-green irises with big dark pupils and bright specular highlights, giving an earnest, slightly wide-eyed stare. His mouth is a simple thin line low on the face — a flat, mildly concerned resting expression that flips easily into a broad, endearing closed-mouth smile when he is happy.

Personality on screen: earnest and eager to help (he loves offering things with outstretched little arms), mildly anxious but good-natured, and quietly celebratory — small hops, claps, waves, and thumbs-up gestures suit him. Movement should be gentle, bouncy, and unhurried.

## Background notes (not for prompting)

- The character originates as "Froge," the mascot of a 2014 mobile game by Fandom Inc.; no individual artist is credited in available sources. It spread as Discord emotes ("Worry Frog") around 2020, then as the "Bufo" Slack emoji pack in tech workplaces.
- Sources interchangeably call him a frog or toad; "Bufo" is the genus of true toads, and the squat body matches the toad framing.
- Emoji variant names (bufo-offers-a-taco, bufo-thumbs-up, bufo-clap, bufo-worried, bufo-happy, bufo-coding, bufo-drinking-coffee) document the character's range: helpful, reactive, workplace-flavored.
- Visual details above were confirmed by direct inspection of the canonical `bufo.png` and `bufo-happy.png` from the all-the-bufo repository, including pixel-sampled colors; hex values are approximate dominant tones, not an official palette.
